Privacy PolicyContact support

Medical notice: PillLog is a personal record and reminder tool. It does not calculate doses, check interactions, diagnose conditions, or provide medical advice. Consult a doctor or pharmacist before making medication decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my medication records stored?

Records are stored on your device and excluded from iCloud Backup. PillLog has no account or application server.

How do I restore PillLog Pro?

Open Settings in PillLog and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Open PillLog Settings and check notification permission. Also check iOS Settings → Notifications → PillLog. Every-few-days reminders are refreshed when PillLog is opened.

How do I export records?

Open History and choose CSV or PDF. Export remains available without PillLog Pro.

How do I report a problem?

Use the support form. Do not include medication records or other sensitive health information.
