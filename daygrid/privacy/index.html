Daygrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Data stored on your device

Daygrid stores the photos you select, captions, dates, and purchase status on your device. Photos are copied into the app's private storage so the journal remains available if an original is removed from Photos.

Data collection and sharing

Daygrid has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or application server. The developer does not collect or receive your photos, captions, or usage data and does not sell or share that information with third parties.

Apple services

Purchases and restoration use Apple's StoreKit service. Camera and photo access are used only when you choose those features. Sharing an export uses Apple's system share sheet and occurs only when you initiate it. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Journal entries remain until you delete them or uninstall Daygrid. You can export photos stored by Daygrid from Settings. Deleting an entry removes its app-managed photo and thumbnail.

Your choices

You can revoke camera or photo access in iOS Settings. Because Daygrid has no account or server, there is no server-side personal data to access or delete.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the Daygrid privacy contact form. Do not include private photos or other sensitive information.
