Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos stored?

Photos and captions are stored in Daygrid's private storage on your device. Daygrid has no account or application server.

How do I restore Daygrid Pro?

Open Settings in Daygrid and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

How do I export my photos?

Open Settings and choose Export all original photos. This feature is available without Daygrid Pro.

How do I report a problem?

Use the support form. Do not include private photos or other sensitive information.
