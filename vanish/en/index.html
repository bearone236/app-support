Vanish — Infinite Tic Tac Toe

Tic tac toe with one new rule: you only keep 3 pieces. Place a 4th, and your oldest one vanishes.

Download on the App Store (free, iPhone) Privacy policy Contact

The vanish rule

Each player can have at most 3 pieces on the board. When you place your 4th piece, your oldest piece is removed. A line you are building can fall apart as pieces vanish, so you have to plan which piece disappears next — yours and your opponent's.

This variant is also called infinite tic tac toe, vanishing tic tac toe or 3-piece tic tac toe. In the UK and Australia, tic tac toe is known as noughts and crosses.

Why it is more fun than classic tic tac toe

Classic 3×3 tic tac toe almost always ends in a draw between experienced players. In Vanish the board never fills up, so draws are rare and the game is decided by reading a few moves ahead.

Features

  • 4 AI levels — Auto (adapts to your results), Easy, Normal and Hard. You can also change how fast the AI moves.
  • Local 2-player — pass one iPhone back and forth.
  • Quick match or Best of 3, with a coin toss for the first move.
  • Progression — XP and coins from every match, 3 daily missions, a daily chest, and 6 ranks from Beginner to Master.
  • Hints and undo — up to 3 hints and 1 undo per game.
  • Offline — no connection needed to play a match.
  • English and Japanese.

FAQ

What is infinite tic tac toe?

A tic tac toe variant where each player keeps only 3 pieces. When you place a 4th, your oldest piece disappears, so the board never fills up and the game continues until someone gets three in a row.

Is there an iPhone app for tic tac toe where pieces disappear?

Yes — Vanish is a free iPhone game built around this rule, with AI opponents and local 2-player mode.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Matches against the AI and local 2-player games work without an internet connection.

Is Vanish free? Does it have ads?

Vanish is free and shows ads, including short ads between some matches and optional reward videos. A one-time purchase removes ads, and you can restore it from Settings.

Is there an iPad or Android version?

Vanish is built for iPhone. There is no Android version at the moment.
